How Each Channel Works

Different intent. Different strategy.

The biggest mistake sparkies make is treating both platforms the same. They're not. Here's the honest breakdown.

Meta Ads — Facebook + Instagram
Interrupt and convert.

Nobody scrolling Facebook is thinking about their switchboard. Your ad has to stop them mid-scroll and make them think about it. Done right, this builds a pipeline of jobs before the phone even rings.

  • Targets homeowners by location, age, property ownership and behaviour — not just search terms
  • Best for higher-value planned work: switchboard upgrades, EV charger installs, rewires
  • Builds brand awareness in your service area so locals think of you first
  • Lower cost per lead than Google for non-emergency work when set up correctly
  • Visual creative that shows the quality of your work before they even call
Google Ads — Search
Catch them when they need you.

Someone types "emergency electrician Brisbane" — they already have a problem and they're ready to call. Google puts you in front of them at the exact moment the intent is highest.

  • Targets people actively searching for electrical services in your area right now
  • Best for emergency and urgent work: power outages, switchboard faults, safety inspections
  • Pays per click — you only spend when someone actually comes to your site or calls
  • Fastest path to booked jobs — results often visible within the first week
  • Local targeting means your budget stays within your actual service area

Research — When to use each

The honest breakdown. Based on real data.

Not theory. Here's what the research actually says about when each platform works, and why it matters for a sparky running a real business.

Meta Ads

Create demand.
Interrupt and convert.

Nobody on Facebook is thinking about their switchboard. Meta shows your ad to people who aren't searching for anything — which means you have to stop them mid-scroll and make them think about their electrical situation before they even knew they had one.

This is called demand creation — planting the seed before they need you so you're the first name they think of when the problem arises.

Google Ads

Capture demand.
Catch them when they search.

When someone types "emergency electrician Brisbane" they already have a problem and they're ready to call. The intent is already there. You're not convincing them they need an electrician — you're just competing to be the one they call.

This is called demand capture — showing up at the exact moment the intent is highest.

The research verdict

Used together, conversion rates increase by over 50%.

Meta builds awareness in your service area so locals recognise your name. When their switchboard plays up, they Google you — and your Google ad is right there. Studies show ads seen across multiple platforms consistently lift conversion rates.

Think of Meta as planting the seed and Google as harvesting it. Together they cover every stage of the job pipeline.

Which One Is Right For You

Every sparky's situation is different.

Situation 01
Solo operator, just starting out

You need leads fast. Budget is tight. You can't afford to wait 3 months for results to build.

Recommendation: Google Ads first. Fastest path to booked jobs. Add Meta once Google is profitable.
Situation 02
Running 2-3 vans, want consistent pipeline

You need a mix of emergency callouts and higher-value planned work to keep all vans busy.

Recommendation: Both channels. Google catches urgent jobs. Meta fills the calendar with upgrade work.
Situation 03
Focused on EV chargers and switchboard upgrades

You're after the $4k to $12k jobs, not $200 GPO swaps. Quality over volume.

Recommendation: Meta Ads. Visual ads targeting homeowners with newer properties hit this market best.
